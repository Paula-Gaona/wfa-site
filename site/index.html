Wolverine Finance Association

The Wolverine Finance Association (WFA) is Utah Valley University’s student finance club and your bridge to a career in finance. We connect UVU students of any major with the industry through guest speakers and panels, career fairs, firm visits, and networking with professionals and alumni, and we help members land internships and full-time roles in investment banking, private equity, asset management, and corporate finance. Members also build the skills firms look for through workshops in financial modeling, valuation, and the Bloomberg Terminal, plus case and stock pitch competitions.
