Wolverine Finance Association

The Wolverine Finance Association (WFA) is Utah Valley University’s student finance club. We help UVU students of any major break into finance with hands-on workshops in financial modeling, valuation, Excel, and the Bloomberg Terminal; guest speakers and panels from industry professionals; case and stock pitch competitions; career fairs and office visits; and recruiting help for internships and full-time roles in investment banking, private equity, asset management, and corporate finance.
